Getting around Singapore

Fast, clean, and easy to navigate — even for first-time visitors.

🚇

MRT

The fastest and easiest way to explore Singapore. Trains run every few minutes and reach almost every major attraction.

🚌

Bus

A convenient way to reach more neighbourhoods and attractions that sit slightly off the MRT network.

🚕

Taxi & Ride-Hailing

Perfect for convenient point-to-point travel, especially late at night or with luggage.

Travel smarter

Get an EZ-Link or contactless bank card — one tap works on MRT and buses.

Avoid the 6–9pm weekday rush on the North-South and East-West lines.

Ride-hailing apps are often cheaper than street-hailed taxis at peak hours.

Most attractions are a short, shaded walk from the nearest MRT exit.
